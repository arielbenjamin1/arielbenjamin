01 · Operations / CRM

Custom CRM & Operations Platform

A centralized system for a home-services business that had been juggling Google Sheets, WhatsApp, a remote secretary and manual processes.

RoleDiscovery, workflow design, UI, build
ToolsBase44, Claude
StatusIn active use · evolving
The problem

Jobs, customers, scheduling and business details lived across disconnected tools. The owner needed one place to manage the day-to-day operation.

The approach

We mapped the lead-to-job workflow together, translated business rules into product requirements, then designed the experience around how the owner actually works.

What it does

Centralizes customer records, job scheduling, technician assignments and workflow stages, with distinct admin and technician experiences.

What’s next

Automated lead capture, SMS/calling, website and WhatsApp integrations, plus a possible migration path into GoHighLevel.

Inside the product

From lead intake to scheduling and daily operations.

About

I like being the person who asks, “Why does this work this way?”

I’m a Richmond-based digital product and marketing professional who gravitates toward the space between people and systems. I like sitting with someone, understanding how they work, spotting the friction, and turning it into something clearer.

That mix of conversation, visual thinking and building is what keeps me engaged. I’m at my best when I can move from understanding the problem to shaping the experience to making a usable first version quickly—then improve it once someone actually uses it.

Outside of work, I’m usually traveling, trying a new tool, helping someone untangle a tech problem, or going down a rabbit hole on aviation, cars or technology.
